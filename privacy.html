Privacy Policy – Cuban Cutz Analytics

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Cuban Cutz Analytics is a private analytics tool used to analyze authorized TikTok account and video performance data for the Cuban Cutz brand.

Data We Access

When the TikTok account owner authorizes the application, the app may access information permitted by TikTok, such as:

  • TikTok username and profile information
  • Public or authorized video information
  • Video performance and analytics data
  • Other information explicitly permitted through the TikTok API

How We Use Data

  • analyze content performance
  • compare videos and performance trends
  • improve future content strategies
  • create reports, recommendations, and analytics for Cuban Cutz

Data Storage

Analytics data may be stored locally on devices controlled by the account owner for historical analysis.

Access tokens and credentials are not intentionally stored publicly.

Data Sharing

Personal data is not sold to third parties.

Data is only processed for the operation and analytics functionality of Cuban Cutz Analytics and through services required to provide the integration, such as TikTok.

Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as necessary for analytics and historical comparisons. Stored data may be deleted when it is no longer required.

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect account credentials and stored analytics information.

User Rights

Users may request access to or deletion of data associated with this application where applicable.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact Cuban Cutz through the official Instagram account: @cubancutz
